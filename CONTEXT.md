# Yah Lah

A daily, mobile-first read of what Singapore is feeling: two short public sentiments a day, chosen by a Singpass-verified electorate.

## Language

**Yah Lah**:
The product's name, which is also the label on the Upvote button.
_Avoid_: SG Pulse (the repository and logo wordmark still use it for now)

**Feeling**:
A short piece of text a Submitter publishes about how they feel, tied to one Hashtag and one Polarity.
_Avoid_: Thought, post, zeitgeist, comment

**Polarity**:
Whether a Feeling is Good or Bad, chosen by its Submitter.
_Avoid_: Sentiment, mood, category

**Hashtag**:
The single word, at most 12 characters, naming the topic of a Feeling (e.g. #lky, #election). Normalised on entry so near-duplicates merge.
_Avoid_: Tag, topic, keyword

**Good Feeling of the Day** / **Bad Feeling of the Day**:
The one Good and one Bad Feeling shown to everyone for a given Cycle, each approved by an Admin from the top Candidates of the previous Cycle's vote for its Polarity.
_Avoid_: Thought of the day, headline

**Carry-over**:
When a Polarity has no approved winner, yesterday's Feeling of the Day stays up, marked "still feeling it".
_Avoid_: Fallback, repeat

**Seed Feeling**:
A Feeling written by the team to fill the first days, labelled "From the team" and carrying no Byline.
_Avoid_: Fake Feeling, sample

## Voting

**Candidate**:
A Feeling that is eligible to win a given Cycle's vote. A Feeling submitted after 21:00 SGT is queued, hidden and not Upvotable, until it becomes a Candidate of the next Cycle.
_Avoid_: Entry, contender

**Rising Candidate**:
A Candidate whose recent Upvote activity is high relative to its total, so it is surfaced beside the top Candidates.
_Avoid_: Trending, hot

**New Candidate**:
A Candidate with hardly any Upvotes yet, given a couple of the places beside the top and Rising Candidates so that fresh Feelings are seen.
_Avoid_: Fresh, recent

**Cycle**:
One Singapore calendar day (00:00 to 23:59 SGT) of Upvoting for its Candidates, ending with the next Feelings of the Day being named.
_Avoid_: Round, day (when meaning the voting period)

**Upvote**:
A User's single vote for one Candidate in a Cycle. Its button is labelled "Yah Lah", the same as the product's name. Can be moved or undone until the Cycle ends. Counts stay hidden until the winner is announced.
_Avoid_: Like, vote

## People

**Visitor**:
Anyone using the site without Singpass login; can read but not Upvote or submit.
_Avoid_: Guest, anonymous user

**User**:
A Singpass-verified person aged 13 or over who can submit one Feeling and cast one Upvote per Cycle. Users cannot contact or engage each other.
_Avoid_: Member, account

**Submitter**:
The User who wrote a given Feeling.
_Avoid_: Author, poster

**Admin**:
The operator who approves Feelings of the Day, reviews Reports, takes down Feelings, and times out Users.
_Avoid_: Moderator, mod

## Presentation and moderation

**Byline**:
What is shown beside a Feeling about its Submitter: initials (first letter of the first two words of their name), sex, and age band (<20, 20s, 30s, 40s, 50s, 60+). For a Submitter under 18 it is sex and "<18" only, with no initials. Never a username.
_Avoid_: Identity, author info, profile, username

**Approval**:
An Admin choosing, before 00:00, one of the top three Candidates per Polarity to become the next Feeling of the Day.
_Avoid_: Sign-off, curation

**Report**:
A User's flag that a Feeling is inappropriate, which hides it from browsing at once and sends it to an Admin. A Feeling still under review at the Cycle's close cannot win.
_Avoid_: Flag, complaint

**Take-down**:
An Admin removing a Candidate or a Feeling of the Day, so the next most-upvoted Feeling takes its place. A User may also ask for a published winner to be removed.
_Avoid_: Delete, ban

**Removal request**:
A User's ask that a Feeling of theirs that has been a Feeling of the Day be removed, archive entry included. The Admin honours it within 24 hours, by a Take-down. Deleting an account does not make one; a User can ask for it in the same go.

**Timeout**:
A one-week default restriction on a User's ability to Report, set by an Admin after they Report an innocent Feeling.
_Avoid_: Ban, suspension

**Election Mode**:
An Admin-controlled state, from the Writ to the end of polling, in which election, party and candidate Feelings are held and their tallies hidden, with a freeze on cooling-off day.
_Avoid_: Blackout, lockdown
